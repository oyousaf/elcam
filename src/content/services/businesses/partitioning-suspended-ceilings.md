## Suspended ceilings

We are suspended ceiling specialists and can install or repair any type, taking a job from the initial design stage right through to installation. We use top quality ceilings and materials from the major manufacturers, in many types of commercial premises and offices.

We can help if your ceiling has been damaged by:

- Burst pipes or leaking water
- Fire or smoke
- General wear and tear

## Office partitioning

If you have an open plan office but would like private rooms or areas, partitioning offers a cheaper and easier alternative to major building work.

We provide a complete range, from basic partition screens to fire-rated performance partitioning and glass partitions, and will prepare a package to suit your visual and budget requirements.
