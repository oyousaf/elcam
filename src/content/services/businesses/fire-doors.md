We are qualified to inspect fire doors, maintain them and install new ones.

Fire doors are a crucial part of passive fire and smoke protection in almost all buildings. They safeguard escape routes and high-risk areas by containing the spread of fire and smoke. Everyday use and general wear and tear mean they need regular checks to stay effective.

## What an inspection covers

A general inspection by a qualified professional includes visual checks of every component of the door:

- Door leaves and frames
- Vision panels
- All gaps
- Ironmongery
- Intumescent strips

## After the inspection

Your survey is then submitted and, if needed, we can prepare a cost breakdown for any defects that need attention. Inspections help identify repairs or remedial work that minimise the spread of fire and smoke and give occupants the time they need to escape safely.

## How often

We recommend checking all fire doors every 12 months, with some doors to lobbies, staircases and plant rooms inspected every 3 months.
