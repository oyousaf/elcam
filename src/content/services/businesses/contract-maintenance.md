When something is damaged in your office or commercial property, it needs repairing quickly. Left alone, it could put you on the wrong side of health and safety rules, or worse, injure someone.

With a maintenance contract you don't need to order building supplies, arrange tradesmen or chase quotes. We visit your premises regularly, listing and repairing faults and damaged areas. We work flexible hours to fit around your office, with as little disruption as possible, on long or short-term contracts.
