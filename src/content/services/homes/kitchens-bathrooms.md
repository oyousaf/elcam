The time and labour involved in renovating a kitchen or bathroom can be reason enough to put it off for months, or even years. Bring in Elcam and you avoid the stress and hassle, and benefit from the knowledge and experience of our building team.

- Ripping out old fixtures and fittings
- Installing pre-packed furniture
- Building and installing bespoke kitchen and bathroom units
- All painting and decorating
